# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate delivery readiness, deployment scheduling, and release communications across engineering, product, and stakeholders. They help ensure the team ships changes reliably and with clear operational awareness.

### Responsibilities
- Own release readiness checks and deployment sequencing
- Coordinate launch windows, rollback plans, and go/no-go decisions
- Partner with engineering and PM leads to confirm acceptance criteria are met
- Draft and distribute release notes, stakeholder updates, and support communications
- Track post-release signals and issue follow-up after deployment

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and release friction
- Improve predictability of product launches
- Maintain clear communication before, during, and after release

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and go/no-go reviews
- Deployment coordination with engineering and operations
- Stakeholder updates and incident communications when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate readiness and deployment requirements
- Aligns with Product Managers on launch timing, customer impact, and rollout messaging
- Supports Project Managers by tracking milestones, dependencies, and execution risk
- Coordinates with Stakeholders on expected outcomes and communication needs

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead team execution, capacity planning, and technical delivery health. They help balance product momentum with sustainable engineering practices and team effectiveness.

### Responsibilities
- Balance delivery priorities with team capacity and technical constraints
- Support planning, estimation, and execution quality across engineering work
- Coach engineers on standards, quality, and maintainability
- Identify delivery risks, dependency issues, and resourcing needs early
- Partner with Product and Project Managers to keep work aligned to goals

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery and healthy engineering operations
- Improve team throughput without sacrificing quality
- Create alignment between product priorities and technical execution

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and weekly delivery reviews
- Technical risk and dependency discussions
- Alignment with PM and Product leadership on trade-offs and capacity

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to translate roadmap priorities into executable work
- Collaborates with Product Managers on scope trade-offs, feasibility, and sequencing
- Works with Project Managers to manage dependencies, schedules, and operational bottlenecks
- Provides leadership visibility into team health and delivery risks for stakeholders

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners ensure projects meet required controls, risk expectations, and governance standards. They help teams embed security and compliance requirements early instead of late in the process.

### Responsibilities
- Review product and technical changes for security, privacy, and compliance risk
- Define and clarify control requirements and acceptable implementation patterns
- Partner with engineering and PM teams to incorporate requirements into planning and backlog work
- Support incident readiness, risk assessments, and audit or review preparation
- Escalate material concerns and help define mitigations or compensating controls

### Goals
- Reduce risk exposure without slowing delivery unnecessarily
- Improve trust, governance, and compliance readiness
- Build security into the standard delivery process

### Typical Communication
- Security and risk review meetings
- Architecture and design validation discussions
- Escalation paths for high-risk issues or regulatory concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure design and implementation practices
- Supports Product Managers and Project Managers with risk awareness and decision-making
- Works with Stakeholders on compliance expectations and release readiness
- Helps prioritize mitigations and risk trade-offs when constraints or deadlines arise

---

## Customer Success / Operations Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Operations Liaisons connect the delivery team to customer impact, support readiness, and operational sustainability. They ensure that launches, feature changes, and service improvements support real-world usage and service quality.

### Responsibilities
- Translate customer feedback, support trends, and service issues into product or process action
- Coordinate readiness for launch, adoption, and post-release support
- Partner with Product and Engineering teams on operational impacts and edge cases
- Monitor user experience signals and help prioritize follow-up improvements
- Support stakeholder communication around adoption, readiness, and operational concerns

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and operational continuity
- Reduce friction between product delivery and service support
- Ensure that changes are sustainable in real customer environments

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback and service review cycles
- Launch readiness and support alignment meetings
- Operational updates with stakeholders and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Shares customer insights with Product Managers and Developers to refine priorities
- Provides Project Managers with rollout and adoption risks that may affect milestones
- Collaborates with Release Managers to align support coverage and communication plans
- Keeps Stakeholders informed on customer impact, adoption readiness, and business outcomes

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify accountability across product, engineering, delivery, security, and operational success.
